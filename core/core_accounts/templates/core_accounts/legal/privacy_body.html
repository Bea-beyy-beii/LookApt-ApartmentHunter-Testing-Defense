Privacy Notice

Effective {{ legal_effective_date|date:"F j, Y" }}

LookApt respects your privacy and processes your personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). This notice explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

When you sign up (everyone)

  • First and last name, email address, mobile number, sex, and whether you are a renter or a landlord.
  • Your password. We only store it in a scrambled (hashed) form, so no one, including us, can read it.
  • The date you agreed to these Terms and this Privacy Notice, and which version you agreed to.

When you use LookApt

  • Renters:
    • saved listings and listings you view (landlords see view counts, not who viewed)
    • applications and waitlist requests: move-in date, site-visit schedule, a note, and the names of the other occupants you list
  • Landlords:
    • your properties: name, address, map pin, prices, features, photos and videos
    • bulletins you post
    • tenancies: tenant and occupant names, rent amount, start/end dates and the rent payments you record
  • Everyone:
    • messages and attachments (photos, videos, PDFs) you send in chat, and when a message was seen
    • reports you file or receive, with any evidence attached
    • notifications we send you
    • your light/dark theme choice
  • Account activity: when you joined and last logged in. If an admin acts on your account, we also keep any warning or suspension and its reason.

Landlord verification (landlords only)

  • A photo of one government ID (e.g. PhilSys, passport, driver’s license, UMID, SSS, PRC, postal, voter’s or senior citizen ID).
  • A selfie of you holding that ID.
  • A business/mayor’s permit or proof of ownership (land title, tax declaration, deed of sale or similar).
  • The result of the check: what the AI read from each document, and the decision.

Government IDs and ownership documents contain sensitive personal information under the law (e.g. ID numbers, birth date). We collect them only to confirm that landlords are real and have the right to rent out their properties, and only with your consent.

One-time codes. When you sign up or reset your password, we email you a 6-digit code. We store only a scrambled version of it. It expires after 5 minutes, and old codes are deleted after a day.

2. Why we use it

  • Create and secure your account, confirm your email and reset your password, using your sign-up details and one-time codes.
  • Show listings and let renters and landlords connect, apply, message and schedule visits, using listings, applications and messages.
  • Help landlords manage tenancies and rent records.
  • Verify that landlords are real and own or manage what they list, using verification documents, which are checked by AI and admins (see section 3).
  • Keep LookApt safe: handle reports, stop scams and abuse, and suspend accounts that break the rules.
  • Send you important emails and in-app notices: codes, verification results, report updates and warnings.
  • Show landlords how their listings perform, using view counts.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Automated checking with AI

LookApt uses an artificial intelligence (AI) system, Claude, made by Anthropic, to check landlord verification documents automatically.

  • What it receives: your ID photo, your selfie, your ownership document, and your first and last name.
  • What it checks: whether each document is readable and genuine-looking, whether the ID is a valid, unexpired government ID, whether the names match your account, whether the selfie shows you holding the ID, and whether there are signs of editing.
  • What it can decide:
    • It can approve a landlord automatically, but only when every check is clear.
    • It can ask you to upload again when a photo is unclear or the wrong document was sent.
    • It never rejects anyone on its own. Anything unclear goes to a LookApt admin, who makes the decision.
  • Your right to a human review: if you disagree with an automated result, email us and a person will review your documents.

Anthropic processes the documents only to give us the result. Under its commercial terms, it does not use them to train its AI models.

4. Our legal basis

  • Your consent, which you give by checking the box at the end of sign-up. This also covers the sensitive personal information in verification documents and the AI check.
  • Providing the service you signed up for (our agreement with you).
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe and preventing fraud.
  • Legal obligations, when a law or a lawful order requires us to keep or share data.

5. Who can see your data

  • Renters see a landlord’s name, “Verified Landlord” badge, number of listings and “member since” date, plus everything in their listings, including the address and map location. Landlords’ phone numbers and email addresses are not shown to renters.
  • Landlords see, for renters who apply to or message them: the renter’s name, contact (email, or phone if there is no email), application details including occupant names, and the messages and files sent to them.
  • People in a chat see each other’s names and messages.
  • LookApt admins can see account details, verification documents, reports and evidence. They use this only to verify landlords, handle reports and support users.
  • Service providers process data for us, only to run LookApt:
    • Supabase (database): all account and app data.
    • Amazon Web Services (S3) (file storage): photos, videos, attachments, report evidence and verification documents. Verification documents are kept in a private folder.
    • Render (website hosting): data passing through the app.
    • Google (Gmail) (sending our emails): your name, email and the email content.
    • Anthropic (Claude AI) (automatic verification check, see section 3): verification documents and your name.
    • Google Sign-In (optional login with Google): your Google email and name.
    • OpenStreetMap, Nominatim and Overpass (maps, address search and nearby places): map locations and searched addresses, not your identity.
    • Google Fonts (website fonts): your IP address when a page loads.
  • Data outside the Philippines: some of these providers store or process data on servers outside the Philippines. We only use providers that protect data with security measures comparable to what Philippine law requires.
  • Authorities: we share data only when required by law, court order or a lawful request, or to protect someone’s life or safety.

6. How we protect it

  • The website uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and one-time codes are scrambled (hashed) and never stored in readable form.
  • Verification documents are stored privately and can only be opened through links that expire after 5 minutes, by you and our admins.
  • Only authorized admins can use the admin tools.

No system is 100% secure. If a breach puts your data at real risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and its data: while your account exists. When it is deleted, your data and uploaded files are deleted with it. The one exception is a landlord’s own record of a past tenancy, which keeps the tenant’s name and the rent history the landlord entered.
  • Verification documents:
    • Approved documents are kept while your landlord account exists.
    • Documents from a rejected request, or ones you were asked to replace, are deleted 90 days after the decision, together with what the AI read from them.
  • One-time codes: deleted after 1 day.
  • Listings, photos and messages: until you delete them or your account is deleted.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used, including automated processing (this notice);
  • access the data we hold about you;
  • correct wrong or outdated data;
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the service, e.g. a landlord can no longer stay verified.
  • erasure or blocking of your data, including deleting your account;
  • data portability: get a copy of your data in a common format;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) if you think your rights were violated;
  • damages, if you are harmed by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or misused data.

To use any of these rights, email {{ support_email }}. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will reply within 15 working days.

9. Cookies and storage on your device

We use only what the site needs to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

  • A session cookie keeps you logged in and remembers your sign-up progress.
  • A security cookie (CSRF) protects forms from attacks.
  • Your browser saves drafts (e.g. an unfinished listing or application) and small preferences, so you don’t lose your work. These stay on your device.

10. Minors

LookApt is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that a minor has an account, we will delete it.

We may update this notice. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app or by email.
